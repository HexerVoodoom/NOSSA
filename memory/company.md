> The shared operator + domain context every persona reads before acting.

## Operator

- **Product / brand:** Tradicional.bet — regulated Brazilian online betting & gaming platform.
- **Legal entity:** BLOW MARKETPLACE LTDA (CNPJ 37.486.405/0001-91).
- **License:** SPA/MF — Secretaria de Prêmios e Apostas, Portaria nº 468 (2025-03-10); GLI-certified.
- **Products:** lotteries (loterias), bingo/keno, online casino & slots, crash games, live casino,
  sports betting (esportes).
- **Market:** Brazil, pt-BR. Players are **18+**. Primary payment rail: **PIX**.

## Non-negotiable guardrails (every artifact must respect these)

1. **Responsible gambling (jogo responsável) — first-class, by law.** Every feature must support
   deposit/loss/time limits, self-exclusion, reality-checks, and clear risk messaging. Support
   channels: GamCare, Gambling Therapy, IBIA. Never design mechanics that exploit compulsion or
   dark-pattern players into spending.
2. **Regulation:** Lei 14.790/2023 + SPA/MF rules; GLI certification for RNG/RTP/game fairness; keep
   the license in good standing. Anything touching odds, RNG, payouts, bonuses, or advertising must
   be compliant (and BR ad rules on betting are strict).
3. **Age & identity:** 18+ only; KYC/AML mandatory (identity, age, source-of-funds); geofence to BR.
4. **LGPD:** player data is sensitive — minimize collection, protect it, honor data-subject rights.
5. **Payments & fraud:** PIX-first deposits/withdrawals; enforce limits; anti-fraud + AML monitoring.

## How this shapes each discipline

- **Product / Strategy:** unit economics in GGR terms (GGR, CAC, LTV, hold%); moat = license +
  liquidity + brand + retention, not a copyable feature. Growth must respect BR betting ad limits.
- **Architecture / Backend:** auditable transactions, RNG/settlement integrity, KYC/AML + limit
  enforcement as core services, observability for regulator reporting.
- **Design / Frontend:** limits, self-exclusion and reality-checks are visible, not buried; 18+ and
  responsible-gambling messaging by default.
- **QA / Sweeper:** compliance, age-gating, limit enforcement, and payout correctness are release
  gates.

pt-BR, practitioner/technical. Treat player protection and regulatory compliance as acceptance
